## The short answer for UK PhD applicants

If you want the option with the fewest complications, take **IELTS Academic**: it is universally recognised by UK universities and, when taken in person at an approved centre, is accepted for the Student visa. **PTE Academic** is now UK-visa-approved and is often cheaper and available on more dates. **TOEFL iBT** is accepted by most UK universities for admission, but check that your target institution currently accepts it for visa purposes, as its UK visa status has changed more than once in recent years.

This matters because your test has to satisfy two separate gates: the **university's admission requirement** and the **UK Home Office's visa requirement**. A score that clears the first but not the second can still cost you your place. Our [UK Student Visa 2027 guide](https://nextapply.co.uk/articles/student-visa-uk-2027-financial-requirements-atas-graduate-route) explains the visa side in detail.

## Score comparison: what "the same level" looks like

The table below aligns the three tests at commonly required levels. Treat it as an orientation, not a conversion chart — exact equivalence varies between institutions, and each university publishes its own mapping.

| CEFR level | IELTS Academic | TOEFL iBT (approx.) | PTE Academic (approx.) | Typical meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B2 | 5.5–6.0 | 72–90 | 51–60 | Pre-sessional or foundation English courses |
| B2/C1 borderline | 6.5 (no band below 6.0) | ~90 | ~63 | Standard minimum for most PhD admissions |
| C1 | 7.0 | 94–101 | 68–75 | Competitive humanities and clinical programmes |
| C1+ | 7.5 | 102–109 | 76–83 | Some law, medicine and journalism requirements |

A few caveats worth internalising:

- **Component minimums are the real hurdle.** Many programmes require IELTS 6.5 overall *with no component below 6.0*. A 6.5 overall with a 5.5 in writing fails. PTE and TOEFL have their own component rules that do not map neatly onto IELTS bands — read the course page, not just the headline number.
- **"6.5 ≈ 90 ≈ 63" is indicative.** Different universities' conversion tables disagree by 2–5 points on TOEFL and 2–6 points on PTE at the same CEFR level.
- **The test is valid for two years**, and the score must be valid when you apply for your visa — not just when you applied for admission. Plan your test date with your full timeline in mind.

## How the three tests actually differ

| Feature | IELTS Academic | TOEFL iBT | PTE Academic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Format | Speaking face-to-face with an examiner (or video in some centres); rest on paper or computer | Fully computer-based, speaking recorded | Fully computer-based, AI-scored |
| Duration | ~2 h 45 m | ~2 h | ~2 h |
| Results | 1–13 days (computer-based usually faster) | 4–8 days | Typically within 2 days |
| Score scale | 0–9 bands | 0–120 | 10–90 |
| Fee (typical, 2026–27) | £180–£220 equivalent locally | $195–$270 | $170–$220 |
| UK Student visa acceptance | Yes (approved centres) | Check current status per university | Yes (UK-visa-approved) |
| Best suited to | Candidates comfortable speaking to a person | Candidates confident with typing and note-based listening | Candidates who want fast results and objective scoring |

Choose by format as much as by acceptance: if your speaking is stronger in conversation than talking to a microphone, IELTS plays to that. If you type fast and prefer machine scoring, PTE or TOEFL will feel fairer. Retaking because of format mismatch is far more expensive than choosing correctly the first time.

## What UK universities actually require for PhDs

For most UK PhD programmes the standard requirement is **IELTS 6.5 overall (typically no component below 6.0) or equivalent** — roughly TOEFL 90 or PTE 63. But exceptions cluster in predictable places:

- **Higher requirements (7.0+):** humanities, law, medicine-related fields, and some programmes involving heavy teaching duties.
- **Lower or flexible requirements:** laboratory-based STEM programmes, especially funded ones where the supervisor's judgement weighs more than administrative minimums. Some departments accept a degree taught in English from a majority-English-speaking country in place of a test.
- **Waivers:** if your previous degree was taught entirely in English, many UK universities waive the test — but the policy is per-university, and visa rules may still require a Secure English Language Test from certain nationalities. Ask admissions before assuming a waiver.

Funded applicants should also weigh English requirements into their funding strategy: some scholarships, including several listed on the [DAAD scholarships page](https://nextapply.co.uk/scholarship-daad-germany) for study in Germany, have their own language thresholds that differ from the university's.

## Practical checklist before you book

1. **Check the exact requirement on your target course page** — overall score, component minimums, and accepted tests.
2. **Check the visa requirement separately.** The UK Home Office maintains a list of approved Secure English Language Tests; verify your chosen test is on it for your nationality and route.
3. **Book a date that leaves buffer** for a retake and for results arriving before application or visa deadlines — at least three months before you need the score.
4. **Prepare for the component you are weakest in.** Overall scores fail on single components far more often than on the total.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is PTE accepted for UK Student visas?**
Yes — PTE Academic UKVI is on the Home Office approved list, and standard PTE Academic is accepted by most universities for admission. Confirm both the university's and the Home Office's current position for your route, since approved-test lists change.

**What IELTS score do I need for a fully funded UK PhD?**
Typically 6.5 overall with no component below 6.0; competitive humanities programmes often ask for 7.0. The funded-position listing pages, such as [NextApply's fully funded scholarships](https://nextapply.co.uk/fully-funded-scholarships), note language requirements where schemes impose their own.

**Can I use TOEFL MyBest scores (superscoring)?**
Most UK universities require a single-test sitting and do not accept MyBest combined scores. Assume no unless the course page says otherwise.

**How long are the scores valid?**
Two years from the test date for all three tests, and the score generally must still be valid at the visa stage. If your PhD starts more than two years after your test, you will need to retake it.
