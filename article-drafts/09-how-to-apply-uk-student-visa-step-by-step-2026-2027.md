**Direct answer box (first 50 words):**

You apply for the UK Student visa online after receiving a CAS from your licensed sponsor university: prepare 28-day financial evidence, get ATAS if your subject requires it, complete the gov.uk form, pay ~£524 plus the health surcharge, attend a biometrics appointment, and wait roughly three weeks for a decision.

---

## Before the visa: the three things you need in hand

The visa application itself is straightforward — it is the preparation that decides the outcome. You need:

1. **An unconditional offer** from a university licensed to sponsor international students, and your acceptance of it.
2. **The CAS** — a digital reference number your university issues once you accept (and usually after paying a deposit). Everything in your application is tied to this number.
3. **Financial evidence** ready before you open the form, because the 28-day holding rule works backwards from your application date.

If you are still at the offer stage, our guide to [applying for a Master's or PhD in the UK](https://nextapply.co.uk/articles/how-to-apply-masters-phd-uk-official-links) and the [12-month application timeline](https://nextapply.co.uk/articles/masters-phd-application-timeline-12-months) will get you here on schedule.

## Step 1 — Check whether ATAS applies to you

Research students in sensitive STEM subjects need an **ATAS certificate** before applying for the visa. Your offer letter states whether it is required. If it is, apply on gov.uk using the CAH3 code from your offer — processing usually takes a few weeks but can be longer. Do this first; nothing else can proceed without it.

## Step 2 — Prepare your financial evidence (start 28+ days early)

You must show maintenance of **£1,483 per month (London) or £1,136 per month (outside London)** for up to 9 months, plus any unpaid tuition fees, held continuously for 28 consecutive days. For the full breakdown of amounts, acceptable documents and common failures, see our [UK student visa financial requirements guide](https://nextapply.co.uk/articles/student-visa-uk-2027-financial-requirements-atas-graduate-route).

The short version:

- Bank statements in your name, or a parent's/partner's with a signed consent letter and proof of relationship
- The closing balance dated no more than 31 days before your application
- No day below the required amount during the 28 days — one dip breaks it
- Scholarship award letter, if your funding covers maintenance

## Step 3 — Complete the online application

Apply on gov.uk up to **6 months before** your course start date (from outside the UK). You will enter your CAS number, ATAS reference, financial details and travel plans, then pay:

- Application fee: ~£524 (main applicant, verify current rate)
- Immigration Health Surcharge: paid per year of your visa — check the rate on gov.uk

Take your time with the form. Small inconsistencies — a start date that differs from the CAS, a name spelled differently from your passport — generate questions at best and refusals at worst.

## Step 4 — Biometrics and documents

After submitting, you book a biometrics appointment at a visa application centre in your country. You will:

- Give fingerprints and a photograph
- Upload or submit your passport, CAS statement, financial documents, ATAS certificate (if applicable), and English language evidence
- Possibly attend a **credibility interview** — a short video interview about your course, funding and study plans. Answer plainly and consistently with your application; "why this university and what will you do afterwards" are the staples.

Most applicants no longer submit physical documents — everything is scanned or uploaded digitally. Keep originals at home in case they are requested.

## Step 5 — Wait, decide, travel

Standard processing takes around **3 weeks**, longer in July–September. Priority services shorten this for a fee. If approved, you receive a decision letter and a digital eVisa. Your 90-day travel vignette (where issued) or digital status lets you enter the UK up to a set period before your course begins — typically one month for courses longer than 6 months.

On arrival, sort your BRP/eVisa access, university registration, and accommodation — see our [UK student accommodation guide](https://nextapply.co.uk/articles/student-accommodation-uk-halls-private-homestay-2027) for how to book halls versus private housing before you land.

## Timeline at a glance

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| 6+ months before course start | Offers accepted; scholarship paperwork settled |
| 3–4 months before | CAS issued; ATAS applied for if required; 28-day funds window planned |
| 6 months–3 weeks before | Visa application submitted online; fee and health surcharge paid |
| After submission | Biometrics appointment within days; credibility interview if called |
| ~3 weeks later | Decision received (standard service) |
| 1 month before course start | Travel to the UK, register, start your course |

## The five most common refusal reasons

1. **Financial evidence fails the 28-day rule** — by far the biggest cause.
2. **Funds held in the wrong form** — fixed deposits, shares or property valuations do not count.
3. **Inconsistencies between the CAS and the form** — dates, course titles, sponsor licence numbers.
4. **Weak credibility interview** — vague answers about your course or post-study plans.
5. **Missing ATAS certificate** for a subject that requires one.

Every one of these is preventable with a week of careful checking.

## Frequently asked questions

**When should I apply for the UK student visa?**
From outside the UK, you can apply up to 6 months before your course start date. Aim for 3 months: enough room for ATAS, biometrics and any administrative delay, without booking flights you might miss.

**How much bank balance is required for a UK student visa?**
Roughly £13,347 (London) or £10,224 (outside London) for a 9-month course at 2026–27 rates, plus unpaid tuition, held for 28 consecutive days. Verify current rates on gov.uk.

**Do I need IELTS for the visa itself?**
Usually no separate test for the visa — your university assesses English as part of admission and vouches for it on the CAS. Some nationalities and circumstances differ. See our [IELTS vs TOEFL vs PTE guide](https://nextapply.co.uk/articles/ielts-vs-toefl-vs-pte-uk-phd-2027) for typical university requirements.

**What is the credibility interview like?**
A short video interview in English about your choice of course, university, funding and plans after graduation. There are no trick questions — consistency and specific, honest answers are what matter.
