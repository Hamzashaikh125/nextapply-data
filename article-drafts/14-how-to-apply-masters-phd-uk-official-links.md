## The application system in one paragraph

The UK has no single postgraduate application system. Taught Master's programmes increasingly use **UCAS Postgraduate**, but many universities still use their own portals — always check the course page. **PhD applications are always direct to the department**, and the real first step is informal: identifying and contacting a supervisor. Everything else — proposals, references, interviews — hangs off that skeleton. Start from our [12-month application timeline](https://nextapply.co.uk/articles/masters-phd-application-timeline-12-months) so the sequencing is right from month one.

## Step 1 — Choose your programmes (and read the entry requirements properly)

Shortlist 4–6 programmes rather than 20. For each, verify:

- **Entry requirements:** a 2:1 UK Bachelor's or international equivalent is standard for Master's; PhDs often want a Master's at Merit level or a strong Bachelor's. If your grades came out of a different system, our [CGPA to UK degree classification guide](https://nextapply.co.uk/articles/cgpa-to-uk-degree-classification) explains how admissions convert them.
- **English requirements:** typically IELTS 6.5–7.0 overall for Master's, 7.0 for competitive programmes; requirements vary by department. Compare tests in our [IELTS vs TOEFL vs PTE for UK PhDs guide](https://nextapply.co.uk/articles/ielts-vs-toefl-vs-pte-uk-phd-2027) — most universities accept all three.
- **Funding status:** a PhD place without funding is a different decision from a funded one. See [how to identify PhDs that are really funded for international students](https://nextapply.co.uk/articles/how-to-identify-funded-phd-accepts-international-students) before you fall in love with a project.

## Step 2 — For PhDs: contact supervisors before you apply

This step separates successful PhD applicants from rejected ones:

1. **Find the right person.** Search department pages for staff whose recent publications genuinely overlap with your proposal — not just keyword matches.
2. **Email them properly.** A short, specific email: who you are, what you propose, why them, and what you have attached. Our [7 email templates for PhD supervisors](https://nextapply.co.uk/articles/how-to-email-phd-supervisor-uk-7-templates) gives you exact wording, and the follow-up guide covers [what to do when supervisors don't reply](https://nextapply.co.uk/articles/how-to-follow-up-phd-supervisor-does-not-reply).
3. **Expect three outcomes:** an invitation to apply formally (good), a redirect to a colleague (also good), or silence (move on after one polite follow-up).

Many UK PhD projects are advertised positions — for those, apply directly to the advert. Reading the advert carefully changes how you should apply: see our [how to read a PhD job advertisement guide](https://nextapply.co.uk/articles/how-to-read-phd-job-advertisement-before-applying).

## Step 4 — Apply through the right portal

- **UCAS Postgraduate (ucas.com):** used by many universities for taught Master's. One profile, one personal statement per choice, application fee applies.
- **University direct portals:** each university's postgraduate admissions page hosts its own form — search "[university name] postgraduate application". Create accounts early; transcripts often need manual upload.
- **PhD applications:** the department's "how to apply" page specifies whether you email the supervisor first or submit to a portal with them named. Follow the stated order exactly.
- **Oxbridge and medicine-adjacent programmes:** earlier deadlines (often October–January) and extra documents. Check the specific course page — these differ from everything else.

## Step 5 — Secure funding in parallel

Do not wait for an offer before applying for funding; most schemes require (or favour) simultaneous applications:

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I use UCAS for a PhD in the UK?**
No. PhDs apply directly to the university department, usually after supervisor contact. UCAS Postgraduate serves taught Master's programmes at participating universities only.

**When should I start applying for 2027 entry?**
Begin researching and contacting supervisors 12–18 months ahead — mid-2026 for autumn 2027. Funded places and scholarships close earliest, often by December or January.

**Can I apply for a UK PhD without a Master's?**
Sometimes — UK programmes frequently accept exceptional Bachelor's graduates directly, especially in the sciences. It depends on the department. Our [PhD without a Master's guide](https://nextapply.co.uk/articles/can-you-apply-phd-without-masters-degree) explains when it works.

**Is it harder for international students to get UK PhD funding?**
Yes for some schemes — many UKRI studentships reserve or limit places by fee status, though fully open international schemes exist and are growing. Target them deliberately using our [identifying funded PhDs guide](https://nextapply.co.uk/articles/how-to-identify-funded-phd-accepts-international-students).

## Official source

Application processes, deadlines and fee statuses vary by university and change each cycle. **Always verify requirements on the official course page (university website) and ucas.com before applying** — this guide orients you, but the course page rules.
